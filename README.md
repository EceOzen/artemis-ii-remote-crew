Artemis II — Remote Crew Mission Log

We are not on the spacecraft.
But we are still part of the mission.

For 10 days, I build one mini-project per day, reconstructing, simulating, and understanding each phase of the mission.

Not just as a spectator—
but as part of the remote crew.

Why This Project Exists

Space missions are not just events. They are systems.

They are:
- trajectories
- models
- approximations of reality

As someone working on simulations—climate, Earth systems, uncertainty—this mission feels familiar. Different scale. Same questions.

How do we model reality?
How close are we?
Where do we fail?
